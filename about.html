ABOUT MANAVTACLASS

Building better learning habits, one step at a time.

ManavtaClass is a learning platform designed to help students understand concepts, practise regularly, assess their progress and improve continuously.

Our Approach

We believe learning becomes more effective when students do more than simply attend classes.

The ManavtaClass learning approach connects four stages: Learn, Practice, Assess and Improve.

For Students

Students can access structured learning experiences, live classes, quizzes, revision resources and other academic support.

For Parents

Parents need visibility into the learning journey. Available monitoring and reporting tools help parents understand learning activity and progress.

Our Vision

To create a learning environment where students develop understanding, consistency, curiosity and the confidence to improve.
